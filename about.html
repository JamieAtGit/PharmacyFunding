Mark Rodgers founded Pharmacy Funding with a clear purpose — to give UK pharmacy owners access to the kind of specialist, personal finance service that the sector has long deserved.

With a career built in financial services and a deep understanding of how pharmacy businesses work, Mark brings a level of sector knowledge that generalist brokers simply can't match. About Pharmacy Funding

Tailored pharmacy finance, from first conversation to completion.

Understanding the unique dynamics of your pharmacy business is central to what we do. We work closely with our clients to gain a detailed understanding of their operations, cash flow cycles, NHS income streams, and growth ambitions — allowing us to deliver tailored, practical funding solutions.

As pharmacies evolve — whether through acquisition, refurbishment, automation or changes in dispensing volumes — their funding requirements also change. We adapt our approach accordingly, ensuring your finance structure continues to support both day-to-day operations and long-term growth.

Along with the Director of the business, Pharmacy Funding has access to a broad spectrum of Associates when more specialist input is required.

We structure funding proposals specifically for the pharmacy sector, presenting your case to lenders in a way that maximises the likelihood of approval. Drawing on extensive experience within financial institutions, we understand how lenders assess pharmacy businesses — from first-time buyers to established groups — and use this insight to your advantage.

We recognise that dealing with lenders can be time-consuming and, at times, frustrating. That's why we manage the process on your behalf — guiding you through each stage, handling communication with lenders, and negotiating terms to secure the most appropriate outcome for your business.

With access to a wide panel of lenders experienced in pharmacy finance, we are well placed to source competitive and suitable funding solutions aligned to your needs.

How we're paid

No fee to you — ever.

We receive payment from the credit provider for introducing you to them. The amount we receive could vary by credit provider and may be higher for certain products compared with others. The payment received may also depend on the interest rate you are charged, as well as the term and product you receive.

Typically, commission received is a fixed fee or a percentage of the amount you borrow. We do not charge you a fee for our services.

Commission disclosure for regulated clients

The Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) expects any intermediary to disclose to the customer that a commission may be payable by the owner or creditor to the intermediary and, if the customer asks, the amount of that commission. The FCA has made it clear that commission means any financial consideration.

All commission received for regulated and unregulated clients is disclosed prior to any transaction taking place within our credit broking agreement, statement of suitability, and Commission Disclosure documents before customer transactions are completed.
